HOW IT WORKS

Starting a game takes one email.

01

Send an email

Email play@yanueslo.resend.app and put your opponent's email address in the subject.

02

Your opponent accepts

They receive an invitation and simply reply with accept.

03

Reply with moves

From that point on, just reply to the same email conversation with moves such as e4, Nf3 or O-O.

04

Chesspost handles the rest

Legal moves are sent to your opponent together with an updated board image. Illegal moves are returned only to you.

STARTING OPTIONS

Optional settings

The email body can be empty. If you want, you can configure the game: 

color: white
delay: 24h

Available colors: white, black, or random.

Without options, Chesspost chooses a random color and delivers moves immediately.

THE BOARD

No chess interface required

Every move email ends with an image showing the current board from your perspective.

Chesspost also keeps track of turns, legal moves, checkmate, draws, resignation and game history.

EMAIL COMMANDS

Everything happens by replying.

e4 Play a move
accept Accept an invitation
decline Decline an invitation
resign Resign the game
offer draw Offer a draw
accept draw Accept a draw
decline draw Decline a draw
rematch Request another game
